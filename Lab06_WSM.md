# Williams-Sonoma, Inc. (NYSE: WSM) — Research Report

**As of:** September 3, 2026  
**Decision and intended user:** This report is for a prospective public-equity investor deciding whether to begin a position in WSM. It uses information available on or before September 3, 2026.

## Final call: Watch / defer

WSM’s operating momentum and liquidity justify continued research, but I would **watch / defer** rather than initiate today. The key reason is not weak reported demand: Q2 FY2026 company comparable-brand revenue rose 6.2% and revenue rose 6.7%. It is that GAAP Q2 profitability includes a material, unusual IEEPA tariff-refund benefit. Management itself presented $2.10 non-GAAP diluted EPS versus $2.84 GAAP diluted EPS for the quarter. [Q2 FY2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/719955/000071995526000208/wsm-20260802.htm) · [Q2 FY2026 earnings release](https://ir.williams-sonomainc.com/investor-information/news-releases/news-release-details/2026/Williams-Sonoma-Inc--announces-strong-second-quarter-2026-results/default.aspx)

**What would change the call:** I would change the call to **initiate** if WSM reports two consecutive quarters of positive company comparable-brand revenue growth and a non-GAAP operating margin at or above its 17.8%–18.2% FY2026 guidance range after the tariff-refund effects have rolled off, and a subsequent valuation provides a margin of safety. [Q2 FY2026 earnings release](https://ir.williams-sonomainc.com/investor-information/news-releases/news-release-details/2026/Williams-Sonoma-Inc--announces-strong-second-quarter-2026-results/default.aspx)

## Company and business model

Williams-Sonoma is a specialty retailer of home products operating a portfolio that includes Williams Sonoma, Pottery Barn, Pottery Barn Kids, Pottery Barn Teen, West Elm, Williams Sonoma Home, Rejuvenation, Mark and Graham, GreenRow, and Dormify. It sells through e-commerce, catalogs, retail stores, and business-to-business channels; management describes the company as digital-first, design-led, and sustainable. [FY2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/719955/000071995526000059/wsm-20260201.htm)

Its potential advantages are a multi-brand portfolio spanning home categories and life stages, in-house design and sourcing capabilities, proprietary customer and e-commerce assets, and stores that complement its omni-channel model. These are company-described competitive attributes, not proof of a durable valuation premium. [FY2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/719955/000071995526000059/wsm-20260201.htm)

## What is going well

- **Demand improved broadly in Q2.** Company comparable-brand revenue grew 6.2%, compared with 3.7% in the prior-year quarter; e-commerce comp was 6.5% and retail comp was 5.5%. Q2 net revenue was $1.960 billion, up 6.7%. [Q2 FY2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/719955/000071995526000208/wsm-20260802.htm)
- **All core brands posted positive Q2 comps.** Pottery Barn was +5.1%, West Elm +6.4%, Williams Sonoma +7.6%, and Pottery Barn Kids and Teen +3.5%. [Q2 FY2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/719955/000071995526000208/wsm-20260802.htm)
- **Liquidity is substantial.** At August 2, 2026, cash and cash equivalents were $1.029 billion; the company reported no borrowings under its revolving credit facility. First-half operating cash flow was $695.9 million, although that includes $200.2 million of tariff refunds and related interest collected. [Q2 FY2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/719955/000071995526000208/wsm-20260802.htm)
- **Management raised its outlook.** Management now expects FY2026 revenue growth of 4.7%–7.2%, comparable-brand revenue growth of 4.0%–6.5%, and non-GAAP operating margin of 17.8%–18.2%. This is management guidance, not an independent forecast. [Q2 FY2026 earnings release](https://ir.williams-sonomainc.com/investor-information/news-releases/news-release-details/2026/Williams-Sonoma-Inc--announces-strong-second-quarter-2026-results/default.aspx)

## What is going poorly or needs normalization

The Q2 GAAP results should not be treated as a recurring earnings run rate. The company recorded a $167.8 million reduction in cost of goods sold for IEEPA tariff refunds and $6.3 million of related interest income. It also recorded $47.5 million to reimburse vendors for prior tariff concessions and a $10.0 million one-time employee-recognition cost; $29.3 million of refund income remained deferred in inventory for expected recognition in Q3. [Q2 FY2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/719955/000071995526000208/wsm-20260802.htm)

Management’s release makes the effect visible: GAAP gross margin was 51.6%, but non-GAAP gross margin was 45.5%; GAAP operating margin was 22.9%, but non-GAAP operating margin was 17.3%; and GAAP diluted EPS was $2.84, but non-GAAP diluted EPS was $2.10. The release attributes the GAAP gross-margin uplift principally to tariff refunds net of vendor concessions, while lower merchandise margins from tariff costs remained a negative 230 basis points. [Q2 FY2026 earnings release](https://ir.williams-sonomainc.com/investor-information/news-releases/news-release-details/2026/Williams-Sonoma-Inc--announces-strong-second-quarter-2026-results/default.aspx)

**Analysis:** The company’s 6.2% comp is evidence of current demand, but the GAAP margin and EPS improvement are not clean evidence of recurring earnings power. The appropriate next test is whether sales growth and non-GAAP margins hold when the refund-related accounting effects disappear.

## Advantages, challenges, and risks

**Advantages.** The Q2 comp gains were broad across the principal brands and both sales channels, while the company held roughly $1.0 billion of cash with no revolver borrowings. The FY2025 filing also identifies its brand authority, product quality, customer service, proprietary customer list, e-commerce capabilities, in-house design, and omni-channel platform as competitive factors. [Q2 FY2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/719955/000071995526000208/wsm-20260802.htm) · [FY2025 Form 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/719955/000071995526000059/wsm-20260201.htm)

**Challenges and risks.** WSM remains exposed to housing conditions, elevated interest rates, consumer confidence and discretionary spending, higher product and transportation costs, global sourcing and supply-chain disruption, tariffs, and foreign-currency movements. The Q2 10-Q also states that tariff uncertainty is expected to continue through FY2026. [Q2 FY2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/719955/000071995526000208/wsm-20260802.htm)

The tariff refund has an external legal and policy backdrop. Reuters reported on August 5 that U.S. customs officials had said about $100 billion in refunds of struck-down tariffs had been completed by the end of July. This helps explain the timing of refund collections, but it does not establish that WSM will receive a repeat benefit. [Reuters report](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-refunds-100-billion-in-tariffs-struck-down-by-supreme-court-filing-shows-4839688)

Recent reporting also describes WSM’s decision to reimburse vendors and make a one-time employee 401(k) contribution from part of the refund proceeds. That reinforces that the Q2 event had an unusual, non-operating character rather than representing normal merchandise economics. [Supply Chain Dive](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/williams-sonoma-to-spread-tariff-refunds-to-vendors-employees/829090/)

## Management outlook and my interpretation

Management says its priorities are to accelerate growth, deliver world-class customer service, and drive earnings. CEO Laura Alber said the company gained market share and raised annual top- and bottom-line outlook; this is management’s characterization. [Q2 FY2026 earnings release](https://ir.williams-sonomainc.com/investor-information/news-releases/news-release-details/2026/Williams-Sonoma-Inc--announces-strong-second-quarter-2026-results/default.aspx)

The FY2026 guidance explicitly assumes that current tariffs remain, oil prices stay elevated, and there is **no** benefit from tariff refunds or related interest. That treatment is appropriate for forecasting discipline and supports excluding the refund from a recurring earnings view. [Q2 FY2026 earnings release](https://ir.williams-sonomainc.com/investor-information/news-releases/news-release-details/2026/Williams-Sonoma-Inc--announces-strong-second-quarter-2026-results/default.aspx)

**Analysis:** The raised non-GAAP guide is more decision-useful than the Q2 GAAP result because management has excluded the identified refund-related effects. It still needs to be tested against future results, especially merchandise margins and demand in the furniture-heavy brands.
